Scholarship Application Guide for Karnataka Students

1

SSP Student Account & Aadhaar

Create your Student Account on the Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP). Ensure your Aadhaar number is seeded and linked with your active bank account for DBT transfers.

2

Caste & Income RD Numbers

Obtain authentic RD numbers (15-digit) from Karnataka Nadakacheri for your Caste/Category and Income certificates. The SSP portal verifies these numbers directly with revenue databases.

3

E-Attestation of Documents

Have your college documents (Bonafide certificate, marks cards, fee receipts) e-attested by your college E-Attestation officer before finalizing your application on SSP.

4

NSP & Central Schemes

For Central schemes (like PM-USP, AICTE Pragati, Saksham), register on the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) with your OTR (One Time Registration) number.
